- Respect boot strapping pins (GPIO 0/2/12 on classic ESP32, GPIO 2/8/9 on C3): no external pulls that fight the straps, and never drive GPIO12 high at reset on 5 V-tolerant modules.

## Recommended Design Rules (What I would actually use)

Humans love designing right at the manufacturing limit and then acting surprised when DFM checks start screaming.

For hobby and professional boards:

| Rule | Recommended |
|--------|--------|
| Trace Width | 0.20 mm (8 mil) |
| Trace Clearance | 0.20 mm (8 mil) |
| Via Drill | 0.30 mm |
| Via Diameter | 0.60 mm |
| Signal Pad Hole | Hole + 0.5 mm |
| Mounting Holes | ≥3 mm |
| Ground Pour Clearance | 0.20 mm |

These values manufacture very reliably and keep costs low.

## ESP32 Projects

For your typical ESP32 + sensors + relays + OLED + RF modules:

- Signal traces: 0.20 mm
- 3.3V power: 0.40 mm
- 5V power: 0.60 mm
- Motor current traces: 1.5 mm to 3 mm depending on current
- Ground plane on both layers
- Via: 0.6/0.3 mm

## If you want the "maximum capability" board

Robu can handle roughly:

- 4-layer boards
- 0.09 mm trace/space
- 0.2 mm drilled vias
- Fine-pitch QFN packages
- BGA down to 0.5 mm pitch with careful fan-out

But for first-pass success I'd stay around:

- 0.15 mm traces
- 0.15 mm spacing
- 0.3 mm drills

That gives fabrication engineers fewer opportunities to curse your Gerbers over their morning tea. citeturn0search0turn0search1
