Cheap. Dramatic. Common.

### 2️⃣ Faulty AC-DC Rectifier Section  
Inside most line-interactive UPS units, AC goes through:

**AC → Bridge Rectifier → Charging Circuit → Battery**

If the bridge rectifier fails, it:
- Still runs in backup mode
- Never charges battery
- Acts like mains doesn’t exist

You’ll usually measure no DC on charging line.

---

### 3️⃣ Charging MOSFET or Control IC Failure  
If the UPS detects AC but doesn’t charge:

- Measure battery voltage while AC is connected.
- If battery voltage does not rise at all → charger section dead.

---

### 4️⃣ Battery Already Dead  
If battery voltage is very low (<10V for 12V battery):

Some UPS units refuse to switch to charging mode.

Measure battery voltage:
- 12V battery should be around 12.5V – 13.8V
- If it's below 10V → likely sulfated/dead

---

### 5️⃣ Control Board Issue  
Microcontroller or sensing resistor failure.  
If AC detection circuit fails, UPS permanently believes power is gone.

Classic symptom:  
✔ Output working  
✖ Charging LED never turns on  

---

1. Measure AC at input.
2. Measure battery voltage without AC.
3. Plug AC.
4. Measure battery voltage again.

- Battery voltage increases → charging working
- Battery voltage same → charger section faulty
