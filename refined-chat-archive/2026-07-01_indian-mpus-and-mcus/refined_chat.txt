## Are there any "100% Indian" chips?

As of mid-2026, **no widely available commercial MCU or MPU is both fully designed and fabricated in India**. Nearly all indigenous processors are **architected and designed in India**, but manufactured at overseas foundries because India is still building advanced semiconductor fabrication capability. Government-backed fabs and new private projects are underway, but commercial high-volume domestic fabrication is still emerging. That distinction matters because designing a processor and manufacturing it are two very different challenges, and India has made much faster progress in the first than the second.
