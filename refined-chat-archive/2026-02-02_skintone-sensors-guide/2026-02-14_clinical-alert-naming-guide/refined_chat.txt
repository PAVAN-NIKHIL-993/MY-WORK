# 1️⃣ Cooling State Alert

### What It Detects
Body temperature dropping below safe range or falling significantly from baseline.

### Physiologic Meaning
Reduced metabolic heat production or excessive heat loss.

### Risk Framing
- Cold stress
- Impaired thermoregulation
- Environmental exposure

### Example Trigger Logic
- Core temp < 35°C sustained  
OR  
- Skin temp drop ≥ 1.5°C from personal baseline within 20–30 min

This is not “hypothermia diagnosis.”  
It’s cooling risk detection.

---

# 2️⃣ Fever State Alert

### What It Detects
Sustained elevation in body temperature.

### Physiologic Meaning
Possible inflammatory or infectious process, or heat stress.

### Risk Framing
- Febrile state
- Systemic stress response

### Example Trigger Logic
- Temp ≥ 38°C sustained 10–15 min  
OR  
- ≥ 1.5°C above baseline for 15 min

Again: pattern detection, not diagnosis.

---

# 3️⃣ Excessive Sweating Pattern

### What It Detects
Rapid or sustained increase in skin microclimate humidity.

### Physiologic Meaning
Active thermoregulation via sweat glands.

### Risk Framing
- Heat strain
- Autonomic activation
- Stress response

### Important Condition
Humidity must be **local skin humidity**, not ambient room humidity.

### Example Trigger Logic
- Humidity > baseline + 15%  
- Sustained > 5 min  
- Optional: HR elevated simultaneously

This becomes your thermoregulatory stress marker.

---

# 4️⃣ Sudden Heart Rate Shift

### What It Detects
Abrupt HR increase or decrease.

### Physiologic Meaning
Autonomic nervous system shift.

### Risk Framing
- Cardiovascular instability
- Stress spike
- Possible presyncopal pattern (if drop)

### Example Trigger Logic
- HR delta ≥ ±15 bpm  
- Within 60 seconds  
- Sustained ≥ 2 minutes  

This is strong and clinically meaningful if implemented with filtering.

No single-sample spikes allowed.

---

# 5️⃣ Low Oxygen Saturation (<95%)

### What It Detects
Reduced peripheral oxygen saturation.

### Physiologic Meaning
Lower arterial oxygen delivery potential.

### Risk Framing
- Hypoxic risk
- Respiratory compromise pattern

### Example Trigger Logic
- SpO₂ < 95%  
- Sustained ≥ 30 sec  
- SQI ≥ threshold  

Never trigger on noisy PPG.

---

# 6️⃣ Physiological Stress Pattern

This is your composite intelligence layer.

### What It Detects
Multi-parameter sympathetic activation.

### Physiologic Meaning
Body under load.

### Example Combined Pattern
- HR > baseline + 15 bpm  
- Temp +0.5°C  
- Humidity rising  
- SpO₂ normal  

### Risk Framing
- Acute stress response
- Heat strain
- Autonomic activation

This is your smart layer.  
It ties everything together without pretending to diagnose sepsis or collapse.
