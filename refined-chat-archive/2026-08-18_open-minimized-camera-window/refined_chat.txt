If you mean **open the camera, keep its window minimized/hidden, but still have it capture photos**, yes. Windows is annoyingly capable of making this harder than it should be.

### Easiest way on Windows 10

If you just want to **take a photo without the Camera window staying in front**:

1. Open **Camera**.
2. Position the camera where you want it.
3. Take the photo normally.
4. Minimize the Camera window with **`Win + ↓`**.
5. The camera app may stop previewing while minimized. **Windows Camera is not designed to reliably capture photos while minimized.**

### If you want this behavior:

> Camera running → window minimized → automatically take photos in the background

Then **don't use the Windows Camera app**. Use a small Python/OpenCV program instead. It can access the webcam while its own window is minimized or even run completely without a preview window.

For example:

```python
import cv2
import time

cam = cv2.VideoCapture(0)

if not cam.isOpened():
    print("Camera not found")
    exit()

time.sleep(2)

ret, frame = cam.read()

if ret:
    filename = time.strftime("photo_%Y%m%d_%H%M%S.jpg")
    cv2.imwrite(filename, frame)
    print("Saved:", filename)

cam.release()
```

That will:

- open the webcam
- capture one frame
- save it as a JPG
- close the camera
- **show no camera window at all**

If your goal is **continuous background photography**, such as **one photo every 5 seconds/minute while the program stays minimized**, that's also straightforward.
