The **SX1278 RA-01SC** is an Ai-Thinker LoRa module operating in the **410-525 MHz** band (typically **433 MHz** in India). The version with the **spring antenna** is compact and easy to use, though it doesn't provide the maximum possible range.

### Spring antenna specifications
- **Type:** 433 MHz helical (spring) antenna
- **Gain:** Approximately **3 dBi** citeturn0search4
- **Impedance:** **50 Ω**
- **Polarization:** Vertical (best performance when mounted upright)
- **Mounting:** Soldered directly to the ANT pad on the module

### Expected communication range

| Environment | Typical Range |
|-------------|--------------:|
| Indoors | 100-500 m |
| Urban outdoors | 500 m-1.5 km |
| Open line-of-sight | **2.5-3 km** |
| Elevated locations | Up to ~3 km |

The official Ai-Thinker specification states that the **spring antenna can achieve about 2.8 km in open environments**, while an external suction-cup antenna can reach around **4.6 km** under similar conditions. citeturn0search2

### If you need more range

Increasing antenna quality gives a much bigger improvement than increasing transmit power alone.

| Antenna | Expected Range |
|---------|---------------:|
| Spring antenna | ★★★☆☆ |
| 16.4 cm quarter-wave wire | ★★★★☆ |
| Magnetic-base whip | ★★★★★ |
| Yagi directional | ★★★★★★ (long-distance links) |

### Important precautions
- **Never transmit without an antenna connected.** The RF power amplifier can be damaged. citeturn0search4turn0search1
- Use a **3.3 V supply only**. The module is **not 5 V tolerant**. citeturn0search2turn0search1
- Place the spring antenna away from metal objects, batteries, and ground planes by at least **10-20 mm** if possible. Humans have an astonishing ability to buy a long-range radio and then wrap the antenna around a battery like it's a decorative bracelet.

Since I know you're designing for maximum performance, I'd recommend **skipping the spring antenna** unless space is extremely limited. A simple **16.4 cm straight wire antenna** or a proper **433 MHz whip antenna** typically gives noticeably better range and radiation efficiency than the compact spring version.
